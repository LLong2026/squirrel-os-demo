## The 3-Tenant Rule

The free plan allows **three isolated tenants** per install. This is deliberate — it's how we run SQUIRL OS ourselves:

1. **Personal** — household automations, bill tracking, personal files
2. **Business** — the operation
3. **Sandbox / Demo** — experiments and training data

Tenant isolation is enforced on every read and every write: one tenant's data can never surface in another tenant's views, logs, or healing events. The free plan lets you verify that yourself.
